Prediction models (no truth input, except the explicitly labelled oracle start):
1. Missing-phase conditional covariance, propagated through the linear solve.
   This is a diagnostic of the tempting shortcut, NOT a theoretically valid
   formula for channel error covariance.
2. Frozen-update covariance: integrate the variance of the Rician posterior
   mean under the current fitted field, hold that field fixed, propagate it
   through the solve. Correct for a fixed, true starting field; ignores the
   dependence of a fitted field on the same observations.
3. Full-estimator sensitivity (primary candidate): central finite differences
   of the COMPLETE unfiltered estimator, including the spectral initializer,
   times the exact Rician magnitude variances at the fitted pre-update field.
   This is a first-order plug-in approximation, not a posterior guarantee.
4. Trace-matched isotropic full-sensitivity control, without channel truth.
5. Proper-complex linear-LS noise covariance (diagnostic, not a phaseless bound).

interleaving that couples antenna rows. Step=1e-4*max(z,sqrt(v)), limited to
0.1*z to preserve positivity. Compare halved steps in preflight and audit.

cross-user correlations. No covariance rescaling fitted to screening truth.
