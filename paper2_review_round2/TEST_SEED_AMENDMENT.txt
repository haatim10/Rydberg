Pre-generation amendment to the fresh-test seed range
8 October 2026

The training protocol's draft final-test seed base, 202610086001 with
increments of 100, overlaps master seeds used for the new diagnostic
phases. This was detected while training was running, before the
training_test directory or any new trained-model test data existed.
No test estimate, error or model choice was available at amendment time.

The actual evaluation base is 20261008000001, with increments of 100.
All 56 master seeds are checked for disjointness against training,
validation, calibration and geometry seed sets. The evaluation manifest
retains both the superseded value and actual value. The original training
protocol and its source hashes remain unchanged; checkpoint selection,
training examples, budgets and all evaluation conditions remain unchanged.
The waiting evaluator was intentionally stopped and restarted before
generation. The training process was not stopped or changed.

A related diagnostic dependency was also found: the tight-pilot P6/SNR5
and weak-reference SNR5 diagnostic cells share a generation master seed.
The first saved channels agree exactly. These two diagnostic phases must
not be treated as independent replication of each other or pooled as
independent samples. Their within-cell paired method comparisons remain
valid. The new trained-model final tests are separate from both phases.
