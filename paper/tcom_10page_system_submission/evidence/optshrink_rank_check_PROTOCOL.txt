FINAL OBSERVATION-BASED OPTSHRINK RANK-SELECTION COMPARISON

Question: does the original188-parameter estimator's trade-off survive an
OptShrink comparator that selects a rank from each world's measurements?
This is a baseline check, not a proposed new selector or universal optimum.
All original sources, models, experiments and the preceding fair comparison
remain frozen. No new neural training and no access to true channel labels.

Freeze this protocol before generating new data. Inherit the same known-noise,
known-reference exact Rician model: N32 K3 P30, spectral initialization, complex
variance convention, PyTorch CPU float64 and one thread. All methods use the
same30 observation columns; no additional pilots. Original learned seeds
9101/9102/9103 are all retained, not ensembled or test-selected.

Rank selection: use the first21 pilots to fit all shared ranks1..15 plus exact
no-filter. Score each on the remaining9 with Rician likelihood. Select the
lowest score, then restart and refit all30 pilots. Each user's channel is
filtered separately but the chosen scalar rank is shared across users, as
in the historical hard-rank comparator. True path count is never supplied.
The mixing strength and iteration budget come from the previously frozen
population-matched validation lookup for known noise/reference level.
Separate in-loop and final-only searches are reported. A joint selector
compares both sets of held-out scores and refits the winner. All search costs
are counted. It does not select a different iteration budget or mixing factor
for each test case; this limitation must remain explicit.

Comparators on the new observations: joint, loop and final rank selection;
previous population-tuned selected/loop/final OptShrink; original narrow-bank
OptShrink; ordinary EM100; one-sweep HS-Rician; three frozen learned models.
No held-out pilot can influence candidate initialization or candidate updates.
After selection all methods have access to the same30 pilot observations.

New data: two independent banks,16 cases in each of126 primary cells and two
separate stress cells,4,096 distinct worlds. The primary grid is seven SNRs
-10:5:20, reference5/12/20dB, six path regimes (random3..7 and2/4/8/12/16).
Stress cells: SNR5 reference0 random3..7, and SNR60 reference12 random3..7.
Master seeds202610610001 and202610710001 plus100per cell. Timing202610810001,
benchmark202610510001, bootstrap202610910001. No old test bank is relabelled as
fresh. Results from the two studies must not be pooled to change a headline.

Primary reporting: equal mean of126 per-cell dB ratio-of-sums NMSEs, average
over the three learned seeds; pooled and mean-case linear error separately.
Also give per-cell, per-SNR, per-reference and per-path errors, worst-cell harm,
full error distributions and fixedNMSE thresholds .5/1/2. Stress not in average.
Paired bootstrap resamples complete worlds within bank and cell and keeps
methods/users/seeds paired. Use Bonferroni-adjusted95% macro intervals for
the three learned-versus-online-rank comparisons,4,000 replicates. Intervals
condition on the trained models and old validation tuning; they do not measure
training-dataset diversity. Cell intervals, if given, are only pointwise.
No success threshold can change after viewing these data. This comparison
tests the accuracy/cost claim; it does not certify novelty or publication.

Time batch1 on one independent case per128conditions, warmup plus three
random-order repetitions. Count both searches for joint selection even when
one result is rejected; include refit and initialization. Exclude model/file
loading. Experimental execution may reuse candidates across reported arms
but standalone reported timings may not share that computation.

Required verification: independent NumPy rank-score/selection/refit checks;
exact bypass; held-out isolation; same rank/iteration/refit definitions;
source and checkpoint hashes; freeze-before-generation; regenerate all data;
replay representative predictions; independent headline recalculation.
Run under a lock with atomic cell saves and deterministic resume. Abort on
numerical failures, never drop cases. Two-hour active-compute cap with saved
partial results if reached. Benchmark on this Mac before quoting runtime.
