TEN-PAGE COMBINED REVISION - 12 OCTOBER 2026

The two unpublished studies now form one journal manuscript, not two
letters. The narrative is: diagnose structural filtering, define a compact
controller, and measure its conditional accuracy-cost trade-off.

MAIN CHANGES
- Simple English, qualitative abstract, background/motivation/contributions
  introduction; no forward section roadmap or research-draft banner.
- Classical array-size and effective-rank evidence is retained with its
  exact model, information references and biased-estimator qualifications.
- The exact Rician update, spectral initialization, numerical guards,
  188-parameter controller and withheld-pilot training remain in the main.
- The primary learned comparison is now the completed, audited compact
  study: 4,032 primary and 64 separate stress worlds, with matched methods.
- The 148-parameter NA-ALISTA-inspired adaptation is described precisely,
  not presented as a reproduction. Its equal 20-epoch budget and continuing
  late validation improvement limit conclusions about stronger training.
- Online-rank OptShrink's accuracy advantage remains visible in the main
  table, discussion and conclusion. Its measured cost includes rank search
  and refitting. Offline training/tuning costs are not included in timings.
- Weak-reference and near-noiseless failures remain in the main table.
- Older detailed protocols, ablations and negative follow-ups are retained
  separately in the supplement rather than pooled or silently discarded.
- Four compact-estimator references were added; HVaF publication metadata
  and the Xu channel-estimation DOI were corrected. EM-GS detection versus
  known-pilot channel-estimation attribution was clarified.

ACTUAL FINAL PRIMARY FINDINGS
Ours: -13.692 dB; recurrent: -11.725 dB; offline OptShrink: -13.204 dB;
online-rank OptShrink: -14.144 dB. Ours gains 1.967 dB over recurrence
(adjusted paired 95% interval [1.932,2.006]). Classical differences here
are descriptive: +0.488 dB over offline, -0.452 dB versus online rank.
Mean one-thread PyTorch CPU times: 4.87,4.15,15.81,110.56 ms, respectively.
The contribution is a specific self-supervised controller and measured
operating limits, not first compact unfolding, new shrinkage optimality,
published NA-ALISTA superiority or universal OptShrink superiority.

STILL UNRESOLVED
- No full-estimator convergence/stability or cubic-shrinkage optimality.
- No hardware, optical/atomic-dynamics or 2D-array validation.
- Known reference/noise in main tests; limited simulation mismatch checks.
- Training with 21 input pilots versus deployment with all 30 is disclosed,
  not isolated by matched retraining.
- Shared training data and fixed models limit the uncertainty assessment.
- No strong matched published URformer or full LRHD/HVaF/LRPR reproduction.
- Author approval and a real permanent public code/data destination remain.

Earlier versions and frozen studies were preserved. The separate supplement
is supporting material for this one paper. This package has not been submitted.
