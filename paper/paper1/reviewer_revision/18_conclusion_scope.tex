The additional comparisons narrow the interpretation of these results.
The original main experiment still gives an average of six paired-median
HS-GS gains of $2.70$~dB, but HS-GS is not the strongest tested structural
estimator in every setting. Smooth singular-value filtering improves on
HS-GS in five of the six new comparison settings with pointwise intervals
above zero. It can also improve on EM-GS where hard-rank HS-GS loses
accuracy. Therefore, the region where HS-GS becomes less useful is a
property of this processing and selection procedure under the tested
conditions. It is not a universal boundary beyond which Hankel structure
has no value. The validation split and deviations from an ideal ULA also
affect the result. We make no claim that the selected rank, the 30\% split
or hard truncation is generally optimal.
