Paper 2 latest review: scientific revision completed, 8 October 2026

The seven author questions are reconciled in RESPONSE_TO_LATEST_REVIEW.txt.
All 25 training jobs, 300 epochs, 56 fresh test conditions (3,584 worlds),
and single-case runtime measurements are complete. The new audit passes
all 2,912 saved prediction files and all 50 selected/final checkpoints.

1. In-loop OptShrink: completed and in the main paper. It wins average dB
   NMSE by 0.143 dB; learned makes fewer severe errors. Broad superiority
   and large speedup over this stronger comparator are not claimed.
2. Trained 5/10/20 stages and 8/16/32 widths: completed. More depth has an
   accuracy/cost/failure trade-off. No optimal depth or convergence claim.
3. Observation-only static gain/noise estimation: completed, with explicit
   costs and failures. Not general calibration recovery or unknown-phase
   recovery. The cancelled measurement-allocation project is not revived.
4. P near 2K: completed with frozen-model transfer. Local full rank does
   not prevent severe finite-noise degradation at six pilots.
5. Weak-reference diagnostics and curriculum: completed. Curriculum gains
   0.206 dB overall but loses 2.353 dB in one cell. Almost-noiseless tests
   expose filtering bias, independently replayed. No universal remedy.
6. Two experts: completed; the 0.023 dB overall gain is smaller than the
   similarly sized width-32 control's 0.065 dB gain. Not a new main model.
7. Fuller reproducibility: saved inputs/predictions/weights, independent
   metric audit, deterministic regeneration and replay tools are included.
   The release manifest is the authority for final packaging verification.

The original model and results remain unchanged. Smaller-budget new
training, previous main results, diagnostics and stronger-baseline tests
remain separately labelled. Training-data variability is reported, not
hidden inside conditional test-case intervals. Seed overlap between two
diagnostic phases is disclosed; the new training test uses fresh seeds.

General IEEE version only. Hardware results, arbitrary calibration drift,
full published LRHD/URformer reproduction, GPU parity, and global
convergence are not established. Venue rules, author details, companion
