Baseline reproduction records for the merged manuscript

These are reporting copies of completed immutable experiments, not new results.
PROVENANCE.json records original repository-relative locations, SHA-256 hashes,
byte sizes, exact-copy checks, source-code hashes, and consistency checks.

filter_continuation_policy.json:
  The 51 compact_choices used unchanged in the final reliability comparison,
  and the 51 repair_choices used by its full repaired receiver comparator.
filter_continuation_protocol.json and filter_continuation_calibration.json:
  The 16-candidate compact menu, historical eligibility calibration, and last
  ten-bin sparse refresh design. Historical timing is not a fresh final time.
dual_guard_policy.json:
  The frozen base beneath shrink_c2_beta1/shrink_c4_beta1. This dependency is
  needed to distinguish learned-start continuation from spectral EM100.
native_runtime_policy.json and native_runtime_protocol.json:
  Verify the numerical optimization retained the complete repair/compact tables.
classical_selection.json:
  All 30 raw-start likelihood scores, each with 126 stratum scores, and the
  selected fixed noise-scaled c=1, beta=1 classical start.
start_ablation_protocol.json:
  The original raw-start selection design. Its later continuation experiment
  was revised before fresh testing; do not interpret its 100-tail setting as
  the final reliability comparator.
start_budget_protocol.json and start_budget_policy.json:
  The common-40 comparator design and retained classical configuration.
policy_summary.json:
  Reporting-only expansion of all 51 compact configs and full-repair bases,
  plus menu, count, calibration, and classical-selection summaries.

The last compact refresh changed only reference12/20 at SNR40/42.5/45/50/60;
the other 41 records remain inherited. The final reliability study performs
no new sparse or classical parameter selection. Neural checkpoint binaries,
large observation banks, and a complete executable dependency tree are not
included here. These records do not turn the manuscript bundle into a full
public experiment release.
