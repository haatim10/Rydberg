Scope decisions for the second review

Implemented: sweep sensitivity, random-mask stability, stored-rank/harm
analysis, cost/memory accounting, shrinkage rationale, pilot-design scope,
verified IEEE related-work addition, and shorter repeated explanations.

The new sweep study retunes each method's parameter using held-out data.
It must not be described as changing only the final projection at fixed
rank. Random partitions are alternative fits, not a newly proposed
multi-fold estimator. Three random masks do not establish universal split
robustness; 100 worlds per setting are focused sensitivity evidence.

Not claimed: global convergence, optimal four-sweep choice, adaptive
stopping, optimal shrinkage, efficient new rank selection, hardware
validation, universal complexity threshold, guaranteed fallback safety,
or attainable improvement equal to the CCRB gap.

Not added: new joint-user/2D Hankel model, unverified IEEE citations,
SURE/AIC implementations without a suitable statistical derivation,
new neural networks, or broader hardware mismatch campaigns. These
would change the research scope rather than finish this revision.
