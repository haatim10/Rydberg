CHANGE AND VALIDATION
Keep the inherited c=4, beta=1 final Hankel filter on spectral EM100 in strong-reference bins 40, 42.5, 45, 50, 60 and above. The same recipe already applies at 37.5. The nearest-bin cutoff was 38.75dB; the filter now remains active across it. Tau is computed from actual supplied noise variance and tends to zero with that variance. No finite high-SNR switch-off remains in this branch; no neural retraining or new parameter search.
Only 30 of 153 grid conditions change. The other 123 are exactly the latest incumbent, including all 18 previous strong-transition conditions and all 51 weak-reference conditions. Ordinary 45 remain exactly original. These are output identities, not statistical noninferiority claims.
One fixed recipe was declared before development, globally accepted after all 30 independent-heldout observable-risk upper95 guards passed. Each guard used 384 anchors/regime, 30 fitting pilots plus 9 independent validation-only pilots, 4000 paired resamples. The bounds are development diagnostics, not simultaneous population guarantees.
The final policy and compact comparator were frozen before fresh test data. Deployment uses 30 pilots. Development never exported true channels. Fresh testing uses 128 anchors per reference/path setting; three fitted neural seeds average individual metrics and do not multiply the sample size. High-branch outputs are fit-independent.
NMSE: equal-cell mean of dB ratio-of-sums NMSE. Main 153-condition grid aggregates exclude 54 added boundary cells. 20,000 coupled channel-index bootstrap resamples per regime. LIMITATIONS
Conditional simulation evidence under the declared channel, reference and noise model; no proof of uniform superiority, BER gain or extrapolation beyond tested 60dB. Threshold scaling is heuristic. Unknown/mismatched noise, finite-iteration EM local solutions, other path distributions and hardware may change performance. Existing lower-SNR routing transitions remain outside this change. The design was motivated by prior observed failures, but the new confirmatory bank and policy freeze are separate from that evidence.
The filter tends toward identity as supplied variance decreases; this alone does not establish exact zero-noise numerical validity. The added boundary points are diagnostic, not extra independently weighted confirmation.
