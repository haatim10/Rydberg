CORRECTED OPTSHRINK CONTROLLER: MATCHED STRONG-BASELINE PILOT
11 October 2026. Separate, preregistered development study; the failed pilot
and its decision remain unchanged. No manuscript changes or novelty claim.

Reason for this new study
The old adaptive gate clipped at full strength, giving zero direct gradient
on many high-SNR cases. With base strength1 its allowed range was only(.5,1].
The old hybrid also used ten updates and a fixed-10 rank lookup, while the
strong comparator used20 updates at15/20 dB and a different rank at20 dB.
This test repairs learnability and matches rank AND iteration schedule.
It does not retrospectively turn the original failed screen into a pass.

Unchanged physical model
Z=|GS+B+W|; N32, K3, P30; known reference and total complex noise variance.
Main cells: SNR -10,-5,0,5,10,15,20 dB; reference ratio12 dB; random3--7 paths.
Stress: weak reference(5dB SNR/0dB reference), two paths(5/12),16 paths(5/12),
nearly noiseless(60/12). Stress cases are not training or tuning cases.
Fresh training1792 worlds(256/cell), validation224(32/cell), test704(64x11).
Seeds: train202610117001, validation202610118001, test202610119001,
timing202610120001; per-cell offset100. No overlap with the previous pilot.
True channels are saved separately ONLY for final evaluation, not training
or selection. Previously inspected banks are development evidence, not reused
as untouched test data here. Source/selection freeze precedes test generation.

Matched rank and update settings, from the frozen strong classical comparator
Noise-level lookup uses 10log10(3/variance), not realized true channel power.
SNR       -10  -5   0   5   10  15  20
rank        1   2   4   5    7   7   7
updates    10  10  10  10   10  20  20
base beta  .5  .5  .5   1    1   1   1
Nearest lookup handles stress/noiseless cases. Every OptShrink variant uses
this exact same rank and update schedule. No hidden extra updates in mixed
batches; finished cases are excluded from subsequent EM/SVD operations.
Ordinary EM100 is a separately labelled diagnostic, not a matched-budget arm.

Arms
1. Strong original OptShrink, unchanged full-strength/half-strength lookup.
2. Validation-tuned constant strengths in{0,.25,.5,.75,1} per noise level.
3. Simple residual rule beta=base*min(1,residual/c); residual is normalized
   squared magnitude error. c in{0,.5,1,2}, with0 meaning fixed strength.
   Tune base jointly on the same strength grid; includes arm2 candidates.
4. Stage-only global learned smooth gate (20 stage parameters).
5. Repaired adaptive gate (8--16--1 MLP,161 parameters).
6. Old clipped adaptive gate, retrained with the SAME strong rank/update
   schedule. This isolates the gate change from the rank/iteration changes.
7. Fixed near-initial gate(.95 where strong base=1, otherwise .5) to isolate
   the slight initialization change. Exact no-filter option exists in arm2.
All arms use the same exact Rician EM and same checked OptShrink operation.

Gate and scope of the repair
New beta=sigmoid(logit(anchor)+u), anchor=clip(base,.05,.95) is a FIXED anchor,
not clipping of learned outputs. At initialization u=0: beta=.5 or .95.
Thus new arms do NOT start exactly at the full-strength classical estimator.
Keep both the unchanged strong baseline and arm7; report epoch-zero outputs.
The new gate can approach zero and one; neither endpoint is reached at finite
mathematical logits. It does not prove safe filtering. Floating-point sigmoid
may still saturate at extreme logits; record beta distribution and gate slope.
Clipped control uses old beta=clip(base+.5*tanh(u),0,1), exact old definition.
Eight inputs unchanged except stage fraction uses each case's actual budget:
effective spectral-rank fraction, leading/tail mass, residual/noise, LS noise
proxy/channel power, reference power/channel power, P/(2K), iteration fraction.
No true rank/channel/error, generator label, or extra pilot observation enters.

Training and selection
Arms global/adaptive/clipped: seeds4201/4202,1792 identical examples,12epochs,
batch32, Adam .001, float64 CPU single thread. No LR search or extra epochs.
Identical example order and21-context/9-heldout pilot masks across arms per
seed. Same fixed independent validation split. Minimize equal-weight main-cell
mean held-out Rician NLL, never channel-error labels. Save initial, selected,
and final checkpoints. Twelve epochs do not imply convergence. All-pilot test
inference uses30; also report21-context diagnostic, not a causal mismatch test.
Classical tuning uses the same validation observations and held-out NLL.
No test-driven repair, retuning, or promotion of the best test seed.

Numerical checks
Independent NumPy parity with the strong baseline at all tested noise levels;
exact per-case update counts in mixed batches; zero-filter equality with EM;
near-initial and clipped-initial equality to their respective fixed controls;
smooth gate range/gradient including strongly positive/negative logits;
nonzero initial end-to-end parameter gradients at every primary noise level;
OptShrink scale/forward checks and separated-spectrum numerical gradients;
full unrolled directional derivatives; deterministic masks and optimizer resume.
Native SVD backward remains unchanged. No detach/identity approximation, hidden
jitter or silently replaced gradient. Exact singular-value ties/guard boundaries
are not claimed smooth. Nonfinite estimates/derivatives stop the run.

Screen and reporting (same thresholds as the failed pilot)
Primary metric: equal mean of seven per-SNR ratio-of-sums dB NMSEs. Retain both
model predictions for paired resampling, not independent-channel pseudoreplicates.
2000 pointwise conditional whole-world bootstrap draws,seed202610122001.
Advance only if adaptive gains>=.3dB versus BOTH strong OptShrink and the
residual control, both lower95% bounds>0, positive mean gain over global,
no primary-cell deterioration>.2dB versus either classical control, and no
mean stress-cell deterioration>.5dB versus either. All failures remain reported.
These are screening thresholds, not publication standards or multiplicity-
adjusted confirmation. Report initial/selected/final, each seed, all cells,
NMSE>1 frequency, runtime, correction size, gate slope/saturation and curves.
No requirement that the new gate beat clipped is hidden: report that contrast
explicitly to assess the repair, but it cannot replace the primary criteria.
