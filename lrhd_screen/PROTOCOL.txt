LRHD adaptation: small matched screen, 15 October 2026
===================================================

Question: does iterating OptShrink/Hankel structure enforcement add useful
accuracy on our magnitude-only receiver, and what does it cost? No training,
change to the original estimator, manuscript edit, or automatic larger study.

Compatibility and limits
------------------------
Yin and Smith, On Low-Rank Hankel Matrix Denoising, IFAC-PapersOnLine 54(7),
198-203 (2021), doi:10.1016/j.ifacol.2021.08.358; arxiv.org/html/2012.07433,
Algorithm 2. Repeatedly apply data-driven singular-value shrinkage and project
onto Hankel structure, re-estimating the singular noise tail each time. There
is no reinjection of the original matrix. Use Pi=I. Stop when the Frobenius
structure discrepancy ||projected-lowrank||/||projected|| <1e-5, separately
for each user; the zero matrix stops immediately. Safety cap 1000 inner sweeps.
Count and report every cap hit; do not describe capped results as converged.

This is an independent complex-valued adaptation from the equations, not an
authors-code reproduction. The authors' code DOI 10.3929/ethz-b-000513984 was
inaccessible at preparation (HTTP429). The paper does not specify a subscript
on its stopping norm, hence our explicit Frobenius convention. Conjugate SVD,
the existing scale-normalized OptShrink D-transform guards, and anti-diagonal
averaging are used. The paper's real additive observations/known true rank
differ from complex, signal-dependent post-EM errors/unknown rank here.
No optimality or convergence theorem is transferred.

Model and unchanged controls
----------------------------
Z=|GS+B+W|, W~CN(0,v); N32 K3 P30, known reference and variance. Reuse the
unchanged ULA simulator, spectral initialization, exact Rician posterior and
linear solve. Filter each user's17x16 channel Hankel matrix, NEVER raw Z.
LRHD enters after each EM step (primary) or after the last step (diagnostic).
An outer mixing strength interpolates the denoised and unfiltered channel.

Original188-parameter model: all three saved validation-selected checkpoints
9101/9102/9103, no retraining or ensemble prediction. Strongest previous
rank-selected in-loop OptShrink: unchanged frozen settings and online21/9
rank selection. Include previous offline-selected OptShrink and ordinaryEM100.
All use the same observations. Every initializer sees only the pilots passed
to its fitting call. Rank fitting uses21; likelihood scoring uses9; selected
rank is refitted from a new initialization using all30. No extra observations.

Small matched development
-------------------------
12 primary cells: SNR{-10,0,10,20}, RSR12, paths{2,random3-7,16} per user.
Eight fresh development worlds/cell (96), master seed20261015201001 with
disjoint case indices across cells. Truth is neither saved nor used for tuning.
Both LRHD and a NEW one-pass OptShrink control use the same candidate grid:
ranks1..15 plus no-filter, strengths{.25,.5,.75,1}, outer budgets{5,20,50,100},
in-loop and final-only placement. Minimum held-out Rician NLL, averaged equally
over the three path populations at each known SNR. Tie order: fewer outer
steps, no filter/lower rank, smaller strength. Choose separately by placement.
Online selection then reselects one shared user rank from the same16 choices
using21/9 pilots, retaining each method's own frozen strength and outer budget.
Also retain offline-selected LRHD loop settings as a diagnostic.

Primary LRHD and matched-onepass controls are in-loop. Final-only LRHD is a
secondary diagnostic, not a test-selected alternative. All results reported.
The old strongest OptShrink has a larger historical tuning budget; it stays
as a strong additional control. No claim of globally optimal LRHD tuning.
Development labels, true path count, or true rank never enter inference.
SNR lookup uses10log10(3/v), never realized channel energy. Ties prefer lower
SNR; stress uses nearest development SNR. This screening lookup is not a new
receiver contribution. Previous strongestOptShrink retains its old lookup.

Fresh test and reporting
------------------------
Generate only after selection freeze. Two independent master seeds
20261015211001 and20261015221001,16worlds/cell/bank. 12primary cells plus
weak reference(SNR5,RSR0,randompaths) and nearly noiseless(SNR60,RSR12,random)
stress cells=448worlds. These are paired across all methods. Separate truth
and observation files. Unique master-seed/case indices and saved hashes.
Report equal12-cell average of dB ratio-of-summed squared error to summed
channel power; per-cell scores; per-case NMSE>1; independent model-seed
values; and stress results separately. Do not pool predictions as independent
channels or combine these populations with old126-cell headline metrics.

Paired within-cell/bootstrap across both banks (2000resamples) is descriptive,
conditional on this small tuning set and the three saved models. Preserve
the three model outputs when resampling a world. No confirmatory acceptance
claim from these intervals, especially with only32cases/cell. Report LRHD gain
against original, matched one-pass, and previous rank-selected OptShrink.

Same backend for every timed method: PyTorchCPU one thread,double precision,
batchone. Warm separately and rotate method order over three repeats on one
new world per cell. Time initialization, online selection, inner loops and
refit. Model loading and I/O excluded; offline tuning time reported separately.
Report runtime distributions, not a hardware-independent speedup guarantee.

Verification and resource limits
-------------------------------
Independent NumPy denoiser checks; one-sweep equality to existing OptShrink;
zero/ties/scaling/phase/exact-lowrank behavior; nofilterEM equivalence;
heldout isolation; deterministic replay. Hash historical sources/checkpoints
before/after; verify unique/matched ids and generation-after-freeze. Inspect
cap residuals and finite outputs. Save complete development cases/test cases,
use an exclusive process lock, and require identical manifest on resume.
At most7200seconds per invocation. Benchmark is disjoint seed20261015181001;
timing seed20261015231001. The benchmark checks speed/numerics, not tuning.
No network training, GPU, dashboards, or manuscript changes.
