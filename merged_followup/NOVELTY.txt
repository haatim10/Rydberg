NOVELTY CHECK: WHAT THIS FOLLOW-UP CAN AND CANNOT ESTABLISH
8 October 2026. Initial focused primary-source search, not an exhaustive review.

1. A small controller, self-supervision, denoising between measurement updates,
   and a generic fallback are not individually new ideas.
   URformer: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.12586
   Zero-shot self-supervised learning and held-out stopping:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.07737
   Safeguarded learned convex optimization:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.01880
   Denoising inside phase retrieval:
   https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/wang20r.html
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18967
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09687

2. A statistically justified Rician risk estimate is also not an untouched
   idea. CURE estimates squared-magnitude/noncentrality risk, not channel risk:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.2848
   Do not apply ordinary Gaussian SURE directly to Rician magnitudes or Hankel
   estimates and call it unbiased. The induced channel noise is correlated and
   depends on the current nonlinear estimate.

3. The candidate question is narrower: can measurements distinguish the need
   for structural denoising from the need to stop filtering and recover detail?
   Rank selection (Paper 1), scalar damping (earlier tests), ordinary early
   stopping, and fixed filtering-then-refinement must all be controls. A gain
   caused only by extra EM iterations is not evidence for a new controller.
   The current release-time argmin is deliberately a simple feasibility tool,
   not a claimed new general optimizer. Passing its small screen is necessary
   to investigate further, not enough to claim publication-level novelty.

4. A defensible future contribution would need a specific nontrivial analysis:
   relate a Rician predictive diagnostic to structural bias under explicit
   measurement-geometry conditions, and demonstrate where that relation fails.
   Conditional inverse stability is different from a universal NMSE safeguard.
   For candidate channels fixed independently of validation observations, the
   expected validation NLL difference is the difference of KL risks for those
   observations. This does NOT make it an unbiased channel-error difference.
   Selecting the smallest noisy empirical loss also introduces selection bias.
   Refitting using all pilots removes validation independence for the refit.

5. The implemented finite-release path eventually becomes ordinary EM if it
   is continued. Hence, under assumptions required for ordinary EM (bounded
   iterates, continuity and suitable stationarity conditions), its asymptotic
   stationary-point claims would be inherited, not a new global-recovery proof.
   With fixed positive noise, a smaller likelihood need not give a better
   channel. In the low-noise limit, correct-model identifiability and basin
   assumptions still matter. No theorem for the full learned scheme is added.

6. Decision order: examine this development screen; inspect matched learned
   training curves; stop failed candidate mechanisms; only then freeze a
   focused independent confirmation protocol. Do not enlarge the study or
   add the candidate to the manuscript merely because it has a new name.

The earlier measurement-information correction FAILED its predefined screen
(macro gain -0.288 dB against scalar correction, sparse-family harm 1.056 dB).
It is not revived here. The cancelled calibration project remains cancelled.
Hardware, 2D arrays and non-Gaussian noise are untested in this follow-up.

7. A more substantial alternative to investigate, not an implemented result:
   noise-matched structural estimation. The current smooth filter is chosen
   for stability, while OptShrink is borrowed from matrix denoising. The
   actual Rician update and Hankel duplication do not produce independent,
   equally distributed matrix-entry errors. The specific research question is
   whether modelling those errors improves filtering beyond scalar weights,
   hard-rank selection and ordinary OptShrink on the same observations.

   Prior art must be taken seriously:
   - Whitening and spectral shrinkage under heteroscedastic noise already
     exist: https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.02201
   - More general correlated-noise theory shows simple whitening/shrinkage
     need not be optimal: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.13912
   - Phase retrieval with denoising and uncertainty exchange already exists:
     https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09687
   - Weighted Hankel approximation is established, not a new invention:
     https://imarkovs.github.io/publications.html
   These findings rule out claiming novelty from simply adding a covariance
   weight, whitening a matrix, or transferring an existing algorithm.

   First gate: derive which covariance is actually being modelled. Given a
   fixed field mu and observed magnitude z, let theta=arg(mu), kappa=2z|mu|/v,
   and Rj=Ij(kappa)/I0(kappa). The latent complex field X on the circle |X|=z
   has conditional mean z R1 exp(i theta), conditional variance
   z^2(1-R1^2), and pseudo-variance z^2(R2-R1^2) exp(2i theta).
   These follow from von Mises phase moments. They are NOT the covariance of
   the channel-estimation error. Propagating them through the least-squares
   update and Hankel lift alone ignores error in the current mu and bias.
   Also, weighting the M-step by these moments does not automatically remain
   an exact EM step: the conditional variance term in the standard complete-
   data Gaussian objective is constant with respect to the new channel.

   A small feasibility protocol must compare any estimated error law with
   repeated-noise errors at fixed channels/pilots, at early and late updates,
   including wrong initial estimates. Use true-field moments only as labelled
   diagnostics. Test an identity/isotropic control and the best scalar filter.
   If the estimated covariance does not describe the relevant error, stop.
   Do not build a large network before that check. This differs from the
   failed Fisher-direction correction in what is modelled, but must still
   beat that earlier mechanism and cannot borrow its claimed motivation as
   proof of a new contribution. No new training campaign is authorized by
   this note. No optimality, convergence, novelty or improvement is claimed.
